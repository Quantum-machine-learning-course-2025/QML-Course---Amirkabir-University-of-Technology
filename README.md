## 🧠 Course Overview
This repository contains the course materials, lecture plans, and assignments for the **Quantum Machine Learning** course offered in **Fall 2025** at **Amirkabir University of Technology**.

- **Instructor:** Dr. Negar Ashari Astani  
- **Teaching Assistants:** Nima Yadollahi & Alireza Khalaji  
- **Contact:** [qml.ta2025@gmail.com](mailto:qml.ta2025@gmail.com)  
- **Course Level:** Graduate  
- **Course Duration:** 90 minutes per session  
- **Primary References:**  
  - *Machine Learning with Quantum Computers* — Maria Schuld & Francesco Petruccione  
  - [Download Reference Book (PDF)](http://103.203.175.90:81/fdScript/RootOfEBooks/E%20Book%20collection%20-%202025%20-%20F/AI%20and%20DS/Francesco_Petruccione,_Maria_Schuld_Machine_Learning_with_Quantum.pdf)

## ⚙️ Requirements

Before starting, students are expected to have a basic understanding of:

- **Python Programming**
- **Quantum Computing**

Recommended reading materials:
- [Introduction to Classical and Quantum Computing – Thomas Wong (PDF)](https://www.thomaswong.net/introduction-to-classical-and-quantum-computing-1e4p.pdf)
- [Quantum Computation and Quantum Information – Nielsen & Chuang (PDF)](https://profmcruz.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/08/quantum-computation-and-quantum-information-nielsen-chuang.pdf)

## 🧩 TA Class Schedule

| # | Topic | Delivery Mode | Duration | TA |
|---|-------|----------------|-----------|----|
| 1 | Preprocessing & Data Preparation, Regression (Linear Models) | Lesson & Coding | 90 min | Nima |
