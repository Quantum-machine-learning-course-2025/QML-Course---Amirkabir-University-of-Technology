## Course Overview  
Welcome to the **Quantum Machine Learning** course for **Fall 2025** at **Amirkabir University of Technology** — where quantum mechanics meets artificial intelligence.  

This repository is your central hub for **lectures**, **assignments**, and **resources**.  
Throughout the semester, we’ll explore how quantum systems can redefine how machines learn, compute, and perceive data.  

- **Instructor:** Dr. Negar Ashari Astani  
- **Teaching Assistants:** Nima Yadollahi & Alireza Khalaji  
- **Contact:** [qml.ta2025@gmail.com](mailto:qml.ta2025@gmail.com)  
- **Level:** Graduate  
- **Session Duration:** 90 minutes  
- **Main References:**  
  - *Machine Learning with Quantum Computers* — Maria Schuld & Francesco Petruccione

## Requirements  
Before you enter the quantum realm, make sure you’re comfortable with:  
- **Python programming**  
- **Quantum computing fundamentals**

Recommended background reading:  
- [Introduction to Classical and Quantum Computing – Thomas Wong (PDF)](https://www.thomaswong.net/introduction-to-classical-and-quantum-computing-1e4p.pdf)  
- [Quantum Computation and Quantum Information – Nielsen & Chuang (PDF)](https://profmcruz.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/08/quantum-computation-and-quantum-information-nielsen-chuang.pdf)
